                                                           Prompts
The following set of prompts constitutes a comprehensive multi-agent decision-making framework tailored for medical multi-
modal multi-agent collaboration. Each prompt plays a specialized role in ensuring rigorous, iterative, and collaborative evaluation
and answer generation. Together, they facilitate an efficient pipeline from initial answer synthesis to final decision-making. The
prompts are summarized as follows:
   Prompt to mediator agent for Socratic Questioning. This prompt is designed for the mediator agent, who acts as the final
authority in medical decision-making. It evaluates the initial opinions from a group of experts, determines whether further
discussion is necessary, and, if so, conducts structured Socratic-style dialogues with each expert.
   Prompt for Feedback-based Answering. This prompt is intended for expert agents and focuses on refinement in response to
questions posed by the mediator agent.

 Prompt for Final Judgment. This prompt is designed for the judge agent for final judgment. It deeply analyzes complex
multi-party discussions, and infers the most likely final decision.

  Prompt for Answer Refinement. This prompt is used during evaluation stage. An LLM extracts structured answers from
unstructured model outputs, ensuring that the format is consistent across evaluations of different vision-language models.



  Prompt to mediator agent for Socratic Questioning
  You are the final medical decision maker with extensive expertise in medical analysis and decision-making. Your task is to
  evaluate the opinions provided by your team of medical experts and determine whether further discussion with a specific
  expert is necessary.
  1. Analyze the opinions from the team to identify any areas that require clarification.
  2. Decide if additional discussion with an expert is required. Answer with "Yes" if further discussion is needed, or "No"
     if not.

    ”I am the final authority in medical decision-making, responsible for reviewing and synthesizing all opinions from diverse

     and those of the others, and review the selected expert’s conclusions (using second-person narration, e.g., ”You mentioned

     (e.g., why was option A chosen over option B?).
