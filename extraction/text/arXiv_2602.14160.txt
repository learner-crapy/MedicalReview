D. Training Setup
We implement our SFT and GRPO experiments using the veRL framework. Tables 9 and 10 summarise the training
configurations for GRPO and sub-agent SFT, respectively. Both the single-agent GRPO and the multi-agent supervisor
GRPO settings employ the same training configuration. All GRPO experiments employ multi-turn rollouts with a global
batch size of 16, sampling 8 responses per query, and are trained for 5 epochs. For sub-agent SFT, we use a micro-batch size
of 1, a global batch size of 4, and a maximum sequence length of 32,600 tokens over 4 epochs. The reduced batch size is
necessary due to the substantially longer input sequences, as the sub-agents are prompted with the full text of the scientific
article specified by the supervisor agent in the tool arguments.
